# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and maintain quality standards, develop test strategies, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and user requirements. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure quality gates are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and quality metrics for features and releases
- Validate acceptance criteria are clear and testable
- Lead test automation and quality assurance activities
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Coordinate with development and product teams on testing requirements
- Document known issues and quality trade-offs
- Participate in sprint planning to understand testing requirements early

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Reduce production incidents through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast feedback loops on quality
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog grooming
- Test plan reviews and quality gates
- Bug triage and severity assessment
- Release readiness reviews
- Daily standups during execution phases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews code for testability, collaborates on test strategy, provides quality feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria, validates feature completeness
- **With Project Managers**: Reports quality status, escalates blockers, coordinates release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads shape the technical direction of projects, review architectural decisions, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They provide mentorship and technical guidance to the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in design reviews and architectural decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers and conduct code reviews
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Collaborate with product and project teams on technical feasibility
- Advocate for technical debt reduction and refactoring
- Define technical requirements and integration points

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Build scalable, performant systems
- Reduce long-term technical risk
- Foster a culture of continuous technical improvement

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review and mentoring
- Sprint planning and risk assessment
- Cross-team technical alignment meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews architecture and code, mentors on best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility of features, discusses trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Identifies technical risks, estimates technical complexity, coordinates dependencies

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, prioritization, and approval for project decisions. They remove high-level blockers and ensure alignment between delivery and business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve project scope, budget, and major decisions
- Prioritize competing initiatives and business needs
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate when needed
- Participate in key decision gates (initiation, go/no-go, release approval)
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across the organization
- Enable efficient decision-making
- Support team success through resource and authority provision

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gate reviews (planning, release, retrospective)
- Escalation and blocker resolution
- Executive reporting and visibility

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Receives status updates, provides prioritization and budget decisions, removes escalated blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns on business priorities and success metrics
- **With Developers/Technical Leads**: Provides context during initiation and planning, may participate in decision gates

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads define user experience, create design specifications, and validate usability. They advocate for the end user and ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability studies
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate design solutions with users and stakeholders
- Define accessibility and usability standards
- Collaborate with developers on implementation
- Iterate on designs based on feedback and metrics
- Document design decisions and design systems

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support burden through good design
- Build consistent, usable product experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and handoff to development
- Usability testing and feedback sessions
- Product planning and roadmap alignment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on feature requirements, validates solutions through user research
- **With Developers**: Provides design specifications, assists with implementation questions, validates final product
- **With Project Managers**: Participates in planning, communicates design dependencies and timeline impact

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, manage deployment schedules, and ensure pre-release requirements are met. They bridge development, QA, and operations to enable smooth, predictable releases.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release cycles
- Coordinate with QA to ensure quality gates are met
- Prepare and manage release notes and documentation
- Orchestrate deployment activities and rollout plans
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response
- Ensure compliance and security requirements are met
- Communicate release timelines to stakeholders
- Monitor deployments and post-release stability

### Goals
- Enable smooth, predictable releases
- Minimize release-related incidents and risk
- Maintain clear communication with stakeholders
- Support rapid iteration through efficient release processes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings
- Pre-release readiness checklists and reviews
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Deployment coordination and status updates
- Post-release verification and incident response

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Coordinates code freeze, understands features and changes, manages deployment timing
- **With QA Lead**: Ensures quality gates are met, confirms testing completion, validates readiness
- **With Project Managers**: Communicates release timelines, manages dependencies, escalates blockers
- **With Stakeholders**: Announces releases, manages expectations, provides post-release updates
